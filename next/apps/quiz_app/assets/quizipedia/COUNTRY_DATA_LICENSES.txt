Country atlas data and attribution (snapshot retrieved 2026-10-19)

Country names, source languages, capitals, area, currencies and border base:
mledoze/countries contributors, https://github.com/mledoze/countries
Database license: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://github.com/mledoze/countries/blob/master/LICENSE
country_facts.json is a derived database made available under ODbL 1.0.
Reviewed corrections and their sources are recorded alongside the affected fields
and in docs/rewrite-agents/tasks/ATLAS_53W8/fact-review.md.

Population: World Bank, SP.POP.TOTL, 2024 mid-year estimates.
https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL
World Bank datasets: CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise specified by the dataset.
https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets

Major geographic and resource associations: CIA World Factbook public-domain
US government geographic text, via https://github.com/factbook/factbook.json
Source records and selected field evidence accompany the generation script.
These records are a historical geographic snapshot, not live economic statistics.

Relief underlay

The optional local relief underlay in assets/quizipedia/relief/ is a reduced
JPEG rendering of Natural Earth NE1_50M_SR_W, Natural Earth 5.1.2.
Natural Earth data are public domain and may be used without restriction.
Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-raster-data/
The image is a label-free orientation layer and is rendered below the map's
vector data. It is not intended for navigation or elevation measurement.

Flags: Flagpedia / FlagCDN, https://flagcdn.com/ and https://flagpedia.net/
Downloaded PNGs from https://flagcdn.com/w160/{iso2}.png for offline display.
FlagCDN credits Wikipedia Commons vector originals. National flag designs may
have local usage rules; no team logos or presidential photographs are included.

Curated landmark, culture, scientist, bridge, president and sports associations
contain names, locations and short original relation labels; each item links its
reference. No source article text or portraits are copied into those records.

Country reading cards

Traditions and food cards citing ich.unesco.org adapt the UNESCO Intangible
Heritage List into short Russian and English accounts. The selection,
translations and adaptations of these records are available under CC BY-SA 4.0.
https://data.unesco.org/explore/dataset/ich001/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The local-name data derived from mledoze/countries in country_geography_stories.json
are available under ODbL 1.0 as above. Distances between capital markers are
rounded great-circle distances calculated from the coordinates in capital_points.json.
Its source registry credits Natural Earth and GeoNames. Elevations and place-name
histories cite the individual records in the World Factbook archive.

Historical atlas polygons (historical_shapes_16 to historical_shapes_20):
Bennett, Mutch, Tollefson et al., Cliopatria v0.0.1, Seshat Global History Databank.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13363121 — CC BY 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The atlas selects POLITY features overlapping 1501–2000, simplifies and rounds
geometry, and groups records by century. Years are inclusive. The neutral land
silhouette is derived from Natural Earth (public domain). Event sources are
listed separately for each entry in history_events.json.
