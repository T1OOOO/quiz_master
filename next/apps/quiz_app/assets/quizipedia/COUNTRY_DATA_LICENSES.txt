Country atlas data and attribution (snapshot retrieved 2026-10-19)

Country names, source languages, capitals, area, currencies and border base:
mledoze/countries contributors, https://github.com/mledoze/countries
Database license: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://github.com/mledoze/countries/blob/master/LICENSE
country_facts.json is a derived database made available under ODbL 1.0.
Reviewed corrections and their sources are recorded alongside the affected fields
and in docs/rewrite-agents/tasks/ATLAS_53W8/fact-review.md.

Population: World Bank, SP.POP.TOTL, 2024 mid-year estimates.
https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL
World Bank datasets: CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise specified by the dataset.
https://www.worldbank.org/en/about/legal/terms-of-use-for-datasets

Major geographic and resource associations: CIA World Factbook public-domain
US government geographic text, via https://github.com/factbook/factbook.json
Source records and selected field evidence accompany the generation script.
These records are a historical geographic snapshot, not live economic statistics.

Flags: Flagpedia / FlagCDN, https://flagcdn.com/ and https://flagpedia.net/
Downloaded PNGs from https://flagcdn.com/w160/{iso2}.png for offline display.
FlagCDN credits Wikipedia Commons vector originals. National flag designs may
have local usage rules; no team logos or presidential photographs are included.

Curated landmark, culture, scientist, bridge, president and sports associations
contain names, locations and short original relation labels; each item links its
reference. No source article text or portraits are copied into those records.
