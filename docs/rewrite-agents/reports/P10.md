# P10 — Flutter catalog-to-history vertical flow

Status: **READY**

P10 plus the P10F review remediation are implemented in the owned Flutter
client. The primary route now performs an in-memory guest bootstrap, loads the
public catalog, validates the authoritative attempt against that exact catalog,
submits typed answers one question at a time, persists accepted receipts,
finishes once, validates result alignment, renders server score/explanations,
and loads server history.

The rejected monolithic implementation was split into focused wire-model,
repository, journey-state and journey-page files. `main.dart` is now 474 lines
and contains the app shell, routes and reusable card widgets rather than the
wire stack. The client retains Dio, Riverpod and Go Router with runtime-only
bearer credentials.

P10F findings are closed:

- sealed staged answers serialize exactly as `{option_id}`, `{option_ids}` or
  `{text}`;
- attempt bundle/question/revision/option order and position maps are validated
  without `.single` or forced option lookups;
- receipts persist in journey state and finish history must exactly match them;
- finish is stored before reveals, and a distinct retryable post-finish error
  retries reveals without another finish request;
- catalog/history loading, empty, error and retry states are localized in EN/RU;
- credential/receipt/finish instants require complete UTC RFC3339 values;
- the fake 25-card test is replaced by a deterministic full API/widget journey;
- reveals match finish history one-to-one and in order, including unique
  question IDs, pinned revisions and the loaded catalog quiz ID;
- remote API endpoints require HTTPS, with plain HTTP limited to loopback
  development hosts;
- normal formatting was applied and the subsequent read-only check changed no
  files.

Verification evidence is in `docs/rewrite-agents/evidence/P10/`. Final fresh
results are 37 passing Flutter tests, a zero-change formatter check and
`flutter analyze` with no issues. P11 still owns Web/Android builds and device
verification; none are claimed here.
