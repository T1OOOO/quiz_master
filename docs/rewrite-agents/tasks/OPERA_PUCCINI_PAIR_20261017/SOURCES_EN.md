## 052 — Madama Butterfly: Giacomo Puccini

1. [Archivio Storico del Teatro dell’Opera di Roma: Madama Butterfly](https://archiviostorico.operaroma.it/opera/madama-butterfly/) — identifies Puccini, Illica and Giacosa, the 17 February 1904 La Scala first performance, John Luther Long and David Belasco as literary sources, named characters, and its three-act catalogue entry.
2. [Teatro alla Scala: Giacomo Puccini (educational dossier)](https://www.teatroallascala.org/static/upload/dis/dispensa-turandot-2024.pdf) — identifies the two-act Milan 1904 version and separately lists the three-act Paris version of 28 December 1906, as well as Illica, Giacosa, and Belasco.
3. [Teatro alla Scala: Butterfly libretto](https://www.teatroallascala.org/static/damweb/upload/biblio/doc/LIBRETTI_7751.pdf) — labels this libretto a two-act tragedy; names Puccini, Illica, Giacosa, Long, Belasco, the characters, and Nagasaki as the dramatic setting.

**Caution:** act counts are version-specific: the inspected sources explicitly describe both a two-act 1904 Milan version and a three-act 1906 Paris version, while the Rome archive carries a three-act entry. Nagasaki is the setting of fictional drama, not evidence that its events are historical record.
