## question:music-oct3-051

**Blind answer:** `music-oct3-051-opt-3` — «Джакомо Пуччини».

The Puccini Museum identifies *La Bohème* as Puccini's opera in four scenes, with a libretto by Giuseppe Giacosa and Luigi Illica. Its page also records the contemporaneous dispute with Ruggero Leoncavallo over the same subject; that confirms why the stem must be understood as Puccini's *La Bohème*, as the parenthetical title alone does not uniquely identify a work across all composers.

Source opened: [Puccini Museum — La Bohème](https://www.puccinimuseum.org/en/giacomo-puccini/le-opere/la-boheme/), accessed 2026-10-17.

## question:music-oct3-052

**Blind answer:** `music-oct3-052-opt-4` — «Джакомо Пуччини».

The Puccini Museum identifies *Madama Butterfly* as Puccini's work and separates its creators, source drama, first performance, and version history. Its two-act 1904 Milan first-performance description should not be generalized to every later version: the same page records versions in three acts and in two acts/two scenes.

Source opened: [Puccini Museum — Madama Butterfly](https://www.puccinimuseum.org/en/giacomo-puccini/le-opere/madama-butterfly/), accessed 2026-10-17.
