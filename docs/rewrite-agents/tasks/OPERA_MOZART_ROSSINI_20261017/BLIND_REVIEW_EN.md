## question:music-oct3-053

**Blind answer:** `music-oct3-053-opt-2` — «Вольфганг Амадей Моцарт».

The Digital Mozart Edition identifies *Le nozze di Figaro*, KV 492, as a four-act opera buffa by Wolfgang Amadé Mozart with text by Lorenzo Da Ponte. It separately records the Vienna first performance of 1 May 1786.

Source opened: [Stiftung Mozarteum Salzburg — Le nozze di Figaro KV 492](https://dme.mozarteum.at/DME/libredition/single.php?end=202&idwnma=5677&line=0&v=1), accessed 2026-10-17.

## question:music-oct3-054

**Blind answer:** `music-oct3-054-opt-3` — «Вольфганг Амадей Моцарт».

The Mozarteum Köchel catalogue identifies Wolfgang Amadé Mozart as composer and Lorenzo Da Ponte as text author for *Don Giovanni*, KV 527. It records the first version's Prague performance on 29 October 1787 and a later Vienna version.

Source opened: [Stiftung Mozarteum Salzburg — Don Giovanni KV 527](https://kv.mozarteum.at/de/work/il-dissoluto-punito-o-sia-il-don-giovanni-6065), accessed 2026-10-17.

## question:music-oct3-055

**Blind answer:** `music-oct3-055-opt-4` — «Джоаккино Россини».

The Teatro dell'Opera di Roma archive identifies Gioachino Rossini as composer of the 1816 *Il barbiere di Siviglia*, with a Cesare Sterbini libretto and a Rome Teatro Argentina first performance on 20 February 1816. The year in the stem distinguishes this version from other settings of the Beaumarchais subject.

Source opened: [Archivio Storico del Teatro dell'Opera di Roma — Il Barbiere di Siviglia](https://archiviostorico.operaroma.it/opera/il-barbiere-di-siviglia/), accessed 2026-10-17.

## question:music-oct3-056

**Blind answer:** `music-oct3-056-opt-1` — «Пётр Чайковский».

Tchaikovsky Research documents the composer and the work's full first staging by Moscow Conservatory students at the Maly Theatre on 17/29 March 1879; it separately records the first professional Bolshoi performance in 1881. This distinction prevents treating the Bolshoi staging as the work's first complete production.

Source opened: [Tchaikovsky Research — Yevgeny Onegin](https://en.tchaikovsky-research.net/pages/Yevgeny_Onegin), accessed 2026-10-17.
