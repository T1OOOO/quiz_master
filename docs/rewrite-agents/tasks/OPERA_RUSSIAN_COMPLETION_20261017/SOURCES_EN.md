# Source ledger — Russian opera completion (057–060)

Accessed: 2026-10-17. Every link below was successfully opened in the research client.

## 057 — The Queen of Spades

- [Mariinsky Theatre: The Queen of Spades](https://www.mariinsky.ru/en/playbill/opera_conchall/pdame_gal/) — music by Pyotr Tchaikovsky, libretto by Modest Tchaikovsky after Pushkin, three acts/seven scenes, and the 19 December 1890 Mariinsky premiere.
- [Mariinsky Theatre Primorsky Stage: The Queen of Spades](https://prim.mariinsky.ru/en/playbill/playbill/2026/4/12/1_1700/) — Pushkin source material, the adapted position of Liza in the opera, and the same premiere convention.

## 058 — Boris Godunov

- [Mariinsky Theatre: new Boris Godunov production](https://www.mariinsky.ru/news1/2026/january/34/) — the first 1869 version, its rejection, Mussorgsky’s later revision, and his self-written libretto after Pushkin and Karamzin.
- [Mariinsky Theatre: 145th anniversary of Boris Godunov](https://www.mariinsky.ru/news1/2019/01/25_5/) — 1868–1869 composition, the 1872 revision, and the 27 January (8 February) 1874 Mariinsky premiere.

Limit: 1869, 1872 and 1874 refer respectively to an original version, a revision, and the staged premiere; later editions are not the original 1869 version in the question.

## 059 — The Snow Maiden

- [Mariinsky Theatre Primorsky Stage: The Snow Maiden](https://prim.mariinsky.ru/en/playbill/repertoire/opera/snow_maiden/) — Rimsky-Korsakov’s music and libretto after Ostrovsky, the synopsis and musical elements, and the 10 February (29 January) 1882 premiere.
- [Mariinsky Theatre: The Snow Maiden, 1882 and 1898](https://www.mariinsky.ru/about/exhibitions/rk175/snow_maiden/) — completion in late March 1881 and the first Mariinsky staging.

Limit: the two January/February dates use old and new calendar styles for one premiere.

## 060 — Prince Igor

- [Mariinsky Theatre Primorsky Stage: Prince Igor](https://prim.mariinsky.ru/en/news1/2023/5/05_3/) — Borodin’s composition and libretto work, basis in The Tale of Igor’s Raid, posthumous completion work by Glazunov and Rimsky-Korsakov, and the 1890 Mariinsky premiere.
- [Mariinsky Theatre Primorsky Stage: Prince Igor](https://prim.mariinsky.ru/en/playbill/playbill/2025/5/9/1_1900/) — Borodin’s music and self-written libretto after The Tale of Igor’s Raid, alongside the production’s account of its 1890 canonical version.

Limit: Glazunov and Rimsky-Korsakov contributed materially after Borodin’s death; the question’s original composer credit remains Borodin.
