# Research log — thematic franchises

Accessed: 2026-10-04. This log records sources actually opened before drafting. It is a fact inventory, not an answer key.

## Terminator (first two films)

- Paramount’s *Terminator 2: Judgment Day* page identifies the film as a 1991 science-fiction action/adventure film with a 137-minute running time.
- Its synopsis says John Connor is still a child and is the future Resistance leader.
- The synopsis says a newer, deadlier Terminator is sent back to kill John.
- It says Sarah and John are not alone because the human Resistance has sent another warrior to protect John.
- The cast listing pairs Linda Hamilton with Sarah Connor, Edward/Ed Furlong with John Connor, Robert Patrick with the T-1000, Earl Boen with Dr. Silberman, and Joe Morton with Miles Dyson.
- The credits list James Cameron as director and writer, William Wisher as writer, and Brad Fiedel for music. These are reserve production facts only; the pack will favour story questions.

## Harry Potter (the seven original books)

- The official Marauder’s Map entry says it displays Hogwarts and tracks human and ghostly inhabitants through the Homonculous Charm.
- The map is not deceived by an Invisibility Cloak, Polyjuice Potion, or Animagi.
- Its makers are Remus Lupin, Sirius Black, Peter Pettigrew, and James Potter.
- The four aliases are Moony, Padfoot, Wormtail, and Prongs.
- The map is revealed with the “I solemnly swear…” phrase and hidden with “Mischief managed.”
- The official Polyjuice entry describes a difficult potion that changes a drinker into another human’s physical appearance.
- It says the recipe is in *Moste Potente Potions* in the Restricted Section.
- It limits the potion to human transformations and lists boomslang skin, knotgrass, fluxweed, lacewing flies, leeches, bicorn horn, and a part of the target as ingredients.
- The Hogwarts entry places the school near Hogsmeade in the Scottish Highlands and lists anti-Apparition charms among its properties.
- The Restricted Section entry says a teacher’s written permission is required because it holds powerful Dark Magic.

## Game of Thrones (HBO screen canon)

- WBD’s official press property page was found in search, but direct opening returned HTTP 403 in this environment; it is recorded in `sources.json` as unavailable and cannot support a question by itself.
- HBO’s official series URL also returned HTTP 403 when opened. No HBO-screen-canon question will be drafted until a directly opened official/WBD source supports its precise fact.
- The accessible press-search text only supports the high-level identification: the series was created by David Benioff and D. B. Weiss, based on George R. R. Martin’s books, and has eight seasons. It is not treated as opened evidence.

## Star Wars (screen canon; no Legends)

- The official Luke Skywalker Databank entry identifies Luke as a Tatooine farm boy raised by Owen and Beru Lars.
- It says R2-D2 carried Leia’s stolen Death Star plans and that Luke flew an X-wing under the callsign Red Five in the Death Star attack.
- It states Luke trained with Yoda on Dagobah, left before completing the training, and lost a hand in his duel with Vader.
- It identifies Vader as Luke’s father and Leia as Luke’s twin sister.
- It says Luke led the mission to rescue Han from Jabba and was sent toward the Sarlacc after defeating the rancor.
- It says the Emperor tried to turn Luke during the Endor-era confrontation, while Vader ultimately threw the Emperor down a shaft.
- The official Yoda entry says Yoda lived in exile on Dagobah and instructed Luke after initially concealing his identity as a Jedi Master.
- The official Kylo Ren entry identifies him as Ben Solo, son of Han Solo and Leia Organa, and a former apprentice of Luke.
- It says Kylo killed Lor San Tekka on Jakku while pursuing the map to Luke, and that BB-8 held the map information.
- All Star Wars questions will name their film/episode context where that prevents canon ambiguity.
