## Source integrity findings

- Direct recipe-level coverage is strongest for pizza, paella, carbonara, nigiri, bibimbap, rendang, ceviche, moussaka, fish tagine and pierogi.
- Two cards deliberately retain only qualified cultural/origin language pending a recipe-level source: doro wat and tabbouleh. The parent should route these to independent fact review before canonical import.
- No card claims a universal sole authentic recipe. Variants are presented as documented regional or related forms, not proof of descent.
- The data has 24 cards, within the packet’s requested 20–24 range, across Europe, West/East Asia, Southeast Asia, North Africa, East Africa and the Americas.
