- Several exact URLs could not be read in this review environment: Vietnam Tourism PDF/banh-mi page, Malaysia Tourism, Visit Mexico, Argentina Travel, China Culture, Ethiopia Travel and Destination Lebanon. Those records cannot meet the packet's "source ... support recipe, origin and version" requirement until a readable, recipe-level primary URL replaces them.
