Access date: 2026-10-12. Every cited URL below was opened directly through the research tool. Snippets are short paraphrase-supporting excerpts, not copied article text.

## culture-variety-pilot-01 — cinema

**Claim mapping**

| Claim | Opened primary/archive source | Access result and supporting excerpt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baum's source text uses silver slippers; the 1939 film uses ruby ones; the final shoes used red satin and about 2,300 sequins each. | Library of Congress, [*To See the Wizard*](https://loc.gov/exhibits/oz/ozsect2.html) | Opened. The exhibition says the book's shoes were originally silver, that ruby was chosen for colour photography, and identifies red satin plus 2,300 sequins per shoe. |
| The film uses sepia for Kansas and changes to colour on Dorothy's arrival in Oz. | American Film Institute, [*AFI Movie Club: The Wizard of Oz*](https://www.afi.com/news/afi-movie-club-the-wizard-of-oz/) | Opened. AFI describes the sepia beginning and ending and the transition to colour once Dorothy reaches Oz; it also confirms silver footwear in Baum's books. |

The Library of Congress record is an archive/exhibition using MGM production material and film objects. AFI is the U.S. film institute's own educational archive. The question tests an adaptation decision, not a cast or crew credit.

## culture-variety-pilot-02 — music

**Claim mapping**

| Claim | Opened primary/rightsholder source | Access result and supporting excerpt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hendrix was left-handed and used right-handed guitars upside-down with reversed strings. | Fender, [*Jimi Hendrix Voodoo Child Strat*](https://www.fender.com/products/jimi-hendrix-voodoo-child-strat-journeyman-relic) | Opened. Fender calls him a left-handed player who used a right-handed, upside-down, reverse-strung instrument. |
| Izabella was a right-handed guitar adapted by reversing strings and nut, moving the strap pin, and playing it upside-down; Hendrix used it at Woodstock in 1969. | Experience Hendrix, [*Historic Stratocaster at Woodstock*](https://www.jimihendrix.com/editorial/experience-hendrix-tour-underway-kickoff-in-jimis-hometown-highlighted-by-onstage-appearance-of-historic-stratocaster-he-played-at-woodstock/) | Opened. The official Hendrix rightsholder site lists those modifications and identifies Izabella as the guitar played at Woodstock in 1969. |
| Fender associates Hendrix's best-known Stratocasters with Monterey 1967 and Woodstock 1969. | Fender, [*15 Stratocaster Players for the Ages*](https://www.fender.com/articles/instruments/15-stratocaster-players-for-the-ages) | Opened. The Hendrix section names those festival instruments and dates. |

Fender is the instrument maker and Experience Hendrix is the artist-rightsholder site. The question asks about instrument adaptation, not an award or performance credit.
