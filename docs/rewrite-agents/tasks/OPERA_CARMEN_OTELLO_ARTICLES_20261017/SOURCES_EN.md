## 007 — *Carmen*: Georges Bizet

1. [Opéra-Comique: “À lire avant le spectacle — Carmen”](https://www.opera-comique.com/fr/actualites/a-lire-avant-le-spectacle-carmen) — the company’s historical article says that in 1872 Bizet received the Opéra-Comique commission and persuaded Ludovic Halévy to adapt Prosper Mérimée’s *Carmen*; Henri Meilhac joined Halévy as librettist. It dates the premiere at the Opéra-Comique to 3 March 1875 and identifies Célestine Galli-Marié as the first Carmen.
2. [Opéra-Comique: “Production for rent: Carmen”](https://www.opera-comique.com/en/production-rent-carmen) — the opera house identifies Georges Bizet as composer and Meilhac and Halévy as librettists after Mérimée’s short story. It calls the work an opera in four acts and places its premiere in 1875 at the Opéra-Comique.

**Distractor and source caution:** Verdi, Mozart, and Puccini are all opera composers, but neither Opéra-Comique record credits them for *Carmen*; Bizet is the composer. The drama and its characters are fictional adaptation material, not evidence about real historical events or communities.

## 008 — *Otello* at La Scala (1887): Giuseppe Verdi

1. [Teatro alla Scala: “Otello, una storia scaligera”](https://www.teatroallascala.org/it/media/la-scala-rivista-del-teatro/otello-una-storia-scaligera.html) — La Scala dates the opera’s absolute premiere at its theatre to 5 February 1887. It identifies the project’s principal figures as Verdi, librettist Arrigo Boito, publisher Giulio Ricordi, and conductor Franco Faccio, and names Francesco Tamagno as the first Otello.
2. [Teatro alla Scala: *Otello*, 2026–27 season](https://www.teatroallascala.org/it/stagione/2026-2027/opera/otello-7-dicembre-serata-inaugurale.html) — La Scala describes *Otello* as composed for the theatre in 1887 by Giuseppe Verdi on a libretto by Arrigo Boito. A conflicting *Aida* year appears across La Scala pages and is excluded from article use.

**Distractor and source caution:** Bizet, Mozart, and Puccini are not credited by La Scala for the 1887 *Otello* in the question; the composer is Verdi. This is distinct from Gioachino Rossini’s earlier *Otello, ossia Il Moro di Venezia* (1816): an official Rossini Opera Festival record was found, but its direct retrieval returned 403/timeout, so it is recorded as a retrieval limit and is not used as inspected-source support here.
