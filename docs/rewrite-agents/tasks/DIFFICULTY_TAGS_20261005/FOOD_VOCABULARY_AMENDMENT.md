# Controlled food vocabulary amendment proposal

Root reread all 66 flagged vocabulary-gap questions with every alternative,
the unchanged keyed index and explanation in three batches (23, 23, 20).
The proposed topic groups in `annotations/food-reworked-report.md` describe
real gaps; they must not be replaced with an inaccurate nearby cooking topic.
This document is a proposal, not an applied dictionary or annotation revision.
The published Quizipedia validation revision still uses the 403-tag dictionary.

Proposed additions: `topic:food-products` (foods and dishes),
`topic:edible-fungi`, `topic:restaurant-industry`, `topic:food-senses`,
`topic:culinary-profession`, `topic:space-food`, `topic:food-history`,
`topic:grain-pasta`, `topic:food-law`, `topic:brand-logos`,
`topic:law-regulations`, and `topic:food-safety`. These are safe vocabulary
concepts; per-question public context still requires visible wording or
announced context. Existing country/ingredient tags remain private.

Additional domain proposal: `domain:society` (society, law and institutions).
The cactus penalty, Thai banknote offence, camouflage ban, monument-visiting
rules and driving fines are law topics, not geography simply because the answer
names a country. Geographic identification may additionally justify geography;
it does not replace the legal topic/domain. Historical laws may also carry
history when the question actually asks about a dated event.

Exact annotation corrections to review with the amendment:

- `food_91` visibly names Japan and concerns public-health regulation: add the
  private Japan tag and society domain; do not certify the explanation's fine claim.
- `food_617` visibly describes an American astronaut and apple purée: private
  US/apple tags are relevant, with space-food/history context as supported.
- `food_172` visibly names Hungary: add private Hungary; do not infer a cuisine
  tag solely from a plausible alternative. `food_100` keyed Mexico should be
  discoverable privately by Mexico.
- `food_4` names Spain, rice and saffron; private Spanish-cuisine and saffron
  tags are appropriate if frozen members are available.
- `food_189` visibly specifies maize flour: reserve an accurate private maize
  ingredient concept rather than call it wheat. `food_p5_4` concerns quinoa,
  which is not a true cereal; a separate quinoa ingredient is appropriate.

The existing food-safety, law, medical, ranking, superlative and option-ambiguity
flags stay private and unresolved. The proposal does not certify rare-steak
safety, allegedly eternal honey, colourful old-law stories, restaurant rankings,
or claimed origins. None of those claims changes an immutable raw answer here.
