# Philology editorial annotation — root

Scope: all six frozen Philology packs, 90/90 source questions. Root read complete
stems, every alternative, numeric keys and explanations in three 30-card batches
from `.run/difficulty-tags/words-source.json`. No source question was changed.
The decisions are explicit per-index scores and individual rationales, rather
than reuse of existing numbers or a filename-derived default.

`words-root.json` uses the explicitly amended 403-tag dictionary. Word meanings,
English vocabulary, rhetoric, religious vocabulary, painting/architecture and
social etiquette are differentiated according to the actual question. France
tags are private and limited to the explicitly stated French borrowing context
or the question asking about France; English words do not acquire a country
tag merely because they are English.

Root separated clues from difficulty: the Caravaggio/Caravaggism and
mosaic/mosaic-maker questions score 1 because the keyed word repeats the stem's
root; rare terms with long explanatory keys were not automatically rated 9.
Toyamyrka retains 9 as an obscure unprompted dialectal term among plausible
alternatives. Existing factual/wording concerns are private flags, including
multiple meanings of vertep, the overly broad chiasmus definition, the legal
term abrogation, spelling of Masaccio and categorical etiquette claims.

This is an editorial draft, not independent acceptance or factual correction.
Current structural checks and source preservation remain an integration gate;
the original 4078-question inventory is still the coverage denominator.
