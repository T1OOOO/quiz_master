## Drafting controls

- Text is newly written in concise Russian and English. It does not reproduce textbook paragraphs.
- OpenStax is used as an educational source and is cited by URL. Its pages describe CC BY-NC-SA terms; this candidate does not copy figures, text blocks, or other assets.
- The map deliberately labels `gastric_glands` and `intestinal_glands` as gland groups. It does not claim an exhaustive inventory of microscopic glands or a definitive total number of organs.
- `pancreas`, `ovaries`, and `testes` have both their endocrine and non-endocrine system roles. The posterior pituitary is described as releasing hypothalamic hormones; thyroid calcitonin is not presented as the main adult calcium regulator.
- Source references are assigned at fact/question level. The revised assignment uses `openstax-stomach` for gastric glands, `openstax-intestines` for intestinal glands, `openstax-salivary` for Q14–Q15, and `openstax-female-reproductive` for the uterus and greater vestibular glands/Q23.
- The thymus is presented as a lymphoid organ without an unsupported secretion; the kidneys retain a general endocrine-system role without listing unsupported individual hormone products.
