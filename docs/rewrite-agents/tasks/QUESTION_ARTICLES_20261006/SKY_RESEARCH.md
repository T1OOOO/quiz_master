# Next Atlas batch: constellation articles

Targets from the current catalog: uma,cas,ori,lyr,cyg,cru,sco,leo.
Research opened on2026-10-06; this is an author packet, not accepted reader content.

- [IAU: The Constellations](https://www.iau.org/IAU/IAU/Astronomy-FAQs/Constellations.aspx?hkey=bb9dc841-0618-41b5-ac70-149741062141): officially defined sky areas differ from illustrative star lines; Big Dipper is an asterism within Ursa Major; no standard IAU stick figure. Boundaries approved1928 and published1930. Avoid claiming every constellation line pattern was fixed in1930.
- [NASA: Discovering the Universe Through Orion](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/stories/quick-reads/discovering-the-universe-through-the-constellation-orion/): belt stars and examples of differing distances; avoid treating their apparent alignment as proximity in space. No exact distance should be imported without identifying measurement/date.
- [ESO: Navigating the stars, Southern Cross](https://www.eso.org/public/blog/navigating-the-stars/): Crux, Acrux/Gacrux, Southern Pointers outside Crux. Distinguish the selected four-star pattern in this app from all stars inside official boundaries and from Cygnus Northern Cross.
