## Author's doubts, outcome
- Ahi Şerefettin / Arslanhane: both spellings are in use. UNESCO list/1694 and the Ankara culture directorate write "Aslanhane"; tr.wikipedia and kulturportali write "Arslanhane" (Ahi Şerafettin/Şerafeddin). Item name is acceptable; ok. Dates 1289-90 confirmed on UNESCO.
- Kaçkar-Rize: ok. Wikipedia categories "Landforms of Rize Province", captions Çamlıhemşin/Rize and Elevit plateau, Rize; 3,937 m and national park 1994 confirmed.
- Sinop prison 1887-1997: ok (Wikipedia Sinop; defunct state prison, now museum).
- Sivas Gök Medrese on 500-lira banknote: ok. Wikipedia Sivas says obverse of 500 lira 1927-1939; koleksiyonlar.net: front shows Sivas Gökmedrese and Atatürk, circulation 5.12.1927 to 15.06.1939. 1271 matches the Sivas page (1271-72 Ilkhanid influence).
- Yusufeli height: FIX, DSI says 275 m (Wikipedia 270 m).
- Boztepe lift (Ordu): FIX, municipality gives 2,372 m line, 510 m top station, 8 min; Wikipedia's 550 m / 6.5 min / June 2012 not confirmable.
- Nevşehir testi kebabı: ok. TURKPATENT 3064: Mahreç İşareti, no. 836, registered 09.08.2021, Nevşehir Chamber of Commerce.
- Şeb-i Arus: ok. GoTürkiye page: "night of reunion", Dec 7-17 2021 evenings at the Mevlana Cultural Centre; 17 Dec 1273 death date confirmed on Wikipedia Mevlana Museum.
- Mount Erciyes: ok. 3,917 m, dormant/inactive stratovolcano, Erciyes Ski Resort "one of the largest in Turkey" all on the cited Kayseri page; Kayseri sits at its foot.
- Hat revolution: ok ("23 August 1925", item says August 1925).
- Single-source Wikipedia-only items: all re-read; fixes listed above.
