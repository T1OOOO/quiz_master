## Most serious
1. rome_476: Romulus Augustulus was deposed at Ravenna (Odoacer took it 4 Sept 476), not "entered Rome"; "sixteen-year-old" is shaky.
2. inca_cajamarca: 15 Nov 1532 is arrival at Cajamarca, not "first contact"; capture was 16 Nov.
3. vasco_da_gama: 20 May 1498 is the standard date; 18 May (worldhistory.org) is only a coast sighting.
4. xinhai_1911: revolution started in Wuchang (central China), not "southern China".
5. boyaca why_*: campaign began 1819, not May 1818 (the cited colombia.co says 1818).
6. mansa_musa: "brought back architects" is a contested tradition.
7. Russian: ficciones section name «Хитросплетения» (not «Вымыслы», the book's own title); great_zimbabwe gender agreement; cairo_trilogy part titles unconfirmed.
