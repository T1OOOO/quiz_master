# Turkey history events delta

16 new events, all `country_ids: ["TR"]`, no year clashes with the 89 existing events (so each keeps its "when" quiz question). `python validate.py` (run from repo root) passes.

Sources opened 2026-11-07 (Britannica via built-in browser, ktb.gov.tr): Lydia (Britannica), Ionian Revolt, First Council of Nicaea, Constantine I, Istanbul, Hagia Sophia, Battle of Myriocephalon, Osman I, Battle of Ankara (1402), Tanzimat, Young Turk Revolution, Gallipoli Campaign, Turkey/History (Samsun 19 May 1919, Sakarya, sultanate 1 Nov 1922), Turkey/Declaration of the Turkish republic (29 Oct 1923, caliphate 3 Mar 1924), Turkey/Foreign affairs since 1950 (NATO 1952), ktb.gov.tr 1934 (5 Dec 1934), ktb.gov.tr 1928 (alphabet, not used).

Least sure
- lydian_coinage: sources only say 7th-6th century BCE (Britannica) / "around the 7th century" (Wikipedia); -600 approximate is a midpoint, no exact year.
- osman_ottoman_state: 1299 is conventional (Britannica infobox: sultan 1299-1324); marked approximate and worded as "conventionally dated".
- myriokephalon_1176: Britannica places it "southeast of modern Ankara"; the text avoids stating the modern location.
- hagia_sophia_537: Britannica says built "primarily 532-537"; no dedication day claimed.
- young_turk_revolution: revolt began in Macedonia, constitution restored in Istanbul; pinned to TR as studied in Turkey.

Dropped

- Troy (c. 1200 BCE): date of the war contested.
- Chaldiran 1514, Selim I conquers Egypt 1517 (year clash with Reformation, location Egypt): not Turkey-located.
- Selimiye 1575: UNESCO page opened, no construction years there; Britannica page paywalled.
- Chalcedon 451: verified (Britannica) but trimmed to keep 16.
- Sakarya 1921, abolition of the sultanate 1922: verified, mentioned inside the 1923 event instead.
- Lausanne 1923: year clash with the Republic. Alphabet reform 1928 (verified, ktb 1 Nov 1928): clashes with penicillin 1928.
- Customs Union 1995: ambiguous year (agreed 1995, in force 1996). Dumlupinar / Great Offensive: no verified source page opened.
- Persian Royal Road, Crimean War, Tulip Era, Myriokephalon-adjacent Rum sultanate: not verified.
