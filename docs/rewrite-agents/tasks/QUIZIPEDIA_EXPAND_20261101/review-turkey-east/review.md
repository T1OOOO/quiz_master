## Author doubts, resolved
- Coppersmiths' bazaar: blog says 500 years / father to son; Turkish press says ~400 years / master-apprentice. Fix: hedge to ~400, new source.
- Diş hediği: OK. HDN (21 Oct 2026) plus a second Turkish outlet confirm TURKPATENT registration and the total of 112. Russian name gets the Latin form.
- Ahlat cemetery "largest": OK as attributed. AA also says "largest necropolis in the Islamic world"; grave counts vary (8,000-9,000). It is covered by UNESCO Tentative List entry "Tombstones of Ahlat..." (2000).
- El Aman "largest caravanserai": fix to "often counted among the largest".
- Yakutiye "largest madrasa with enclosed courtyard": OK as attributed, single source (goturkiye); Wikipedia makes no size claim.
- Urfa Man "oldest": fix (oldest naturalistic life-size human sculpture; not oldest sculpture).
- Zerzevan "last Mithras temple": OK as attributed (goturkiye; Daily Sabah headline "Last Mithras temple found at Zerzevan", not opened). On UNESCO Tentative List (14/04/2020, opened).
- Bitlis Castle / Alexander: OK, already hedged ("said to").
- Nemrut Lion Horoscope: fix (date is debated; not clearly the accession; no "oldest").
- Malatya apricot: fix, the EU status is PDO (Reg. 2017/1220, opened), not a generic GI.
- Russian names: "менгич" -> "менегич"; "Урфинский человек" -> "Человек из Урфы"; "Догубаязыт" -> "Догубаязит"; "Бакырджилар" -> "Бакырджылар"; "мыру" -> "мырру"; "Кягыт" -> "Каыт". Confirmed by ru.wikipedia: Дюлюк, Каркамыш, Румкале (all in "Газиантеп (ил)"), Халфети, Зерзеван, Игдыр, Хаккяри, Тунджели, Бингёль, Якутие/Чифте Минарели (Эрзурум).
