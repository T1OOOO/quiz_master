## question:music-oct3-010

**Blind answer:** `music-oct3-010-opt-4` — «Джакомо Пуччини».

Teatro alla Scala credits the music to Giacomo Puccini and identifies Franco Alfano as the person who completed the third act. The Puccini catalogue likewise records Alfano's later selection to complete the work. The stem's distinction between the opera's principal composer and its completer makes Puccini the sole fitting choice.
