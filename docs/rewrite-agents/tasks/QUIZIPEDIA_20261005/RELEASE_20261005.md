## Product scope and limits

The four local, unranked modules contain 20 playable countries over the
177-feature Natural Earth background, six endocrine structures with 14
clickable locations, six real landmark photos, and eight famous constellation
patterns with 56 HYG stars. Explore and quiz directions are implemented.
Sky patterns use fixed J2000 coordinates, not a live location/time sky view.
Sources, creators and individual licenses are accessible in the application.

Independent facts/assets, integration, provider and release-package reviews
are recorded in this directory. Automated rendering/navigation/selection,
zoom/pan, feedback, error and short-phone tests passed. An actual production
browser check remains pending a granted host browser/resource lease; public
HTTP and widget acceptance alone are not claimed as browser acceptance.

The difficulty/tag work in the shared working tree was deliberately excluded
from this release. Existing metadata drafts are not a completed review or
publication of all 4,078 ordinary and Study questions.

Local ignored proof files: `.run/quizipedia/deploy-output.txt`,
`.run/quizipedia/package.json`, `.run/quizipedia/production-smoke.json`.

was needed by the deployment or post-release API/asset checks.
