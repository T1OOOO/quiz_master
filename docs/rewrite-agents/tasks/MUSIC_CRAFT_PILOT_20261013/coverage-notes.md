# Music craft pilot: duplicate and coverage notes

Accessed 2026-10-13. I ran a targeted search across `quizzes/` and `study/`
for Russian stems and article text containing `сэмпл`, `семпл`, `ремикс`,
`акапел`, `вибрато`, `панорам`, `стерео` and `микш`.

- There is no returned released-question match for the four proposed knowledge
  points: placement with a pan control, fixing a late loop attack with a
  warp-marker, a pop filter for vocal plosives, or sampling a recorded fragment.
- The only returned production-adjacent match was the existing film-craft
  question whose distractor says «Панорамировать камеру и снять кадр»; it is
  about stop-motion and not audio panorama.
- Reader drafts contain an existing U2 article that names a historical mixing
  credit, and the film-craft article mentions a camera pan. Neither article
  teaches the four proposed music-craft concepts.
- `metadata/tags.v1.json` was checked. `domain:arts` is an existing,
  player-safe tag and is the sole tag used for every draft item. No new tag is
  proposed.

This was a targeted lexical and semantic screen, not a claim that every
question or Reader article in the repository has been re-fact-checked.
