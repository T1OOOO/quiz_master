## 025 — *It’s a Wonderful Life* (1946): James Stewart — George Bailey

1. [AFI Catalog: *It’s a Wonderful Life* (1946)](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/27682-ITS-AWONDERFULLIFE?cp=1&pos=1&sid=530c79d4-efe1-49c7-92f6-4a3761b6f09c&sr=0.09652613) — AFI credits James Stewart as George Bailey. It identifies Philip Van Doren Stern’s “The Greatest Gift” as the source story and says Stewart’s film was his first after four years of World War II service. AFI also records Liberty Films as Capra and three fellow veterans’ first production and describes the artificial snowfall, which used limestone and white plaster.
2. [Academy: *It’s a Wonderful Life* (1946)](https://www.oscars.org/events/its-wonderful-life-1946) — the Academy identifies George Bailey as Stewart, calls him a Bedford Falls businessman, names Clarence as the guardian angel who intervenes, and says the film drew five Academy Award nominations, including Stewart for Actor, Capra for Directing, and Best Picture. It identifies Stern’s short story as the basis.

**Safe article material:** George, Clarence and Bedford Falls belong to a fictional fantasy narrative. Stewart’s wartime service is production/performer context, not a fact about George. The film adapts Stern’s story rather than documenting a real small town or a real suicide intervention.

## 026 — *The Deer Hunter* (1978): Robert De Niro — Michael Vronsky

1. [AFI Catalog: *The Deer Hunter* (1978)](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/55192-THE-DEERHUNTER?cp=1&cxt=Filmography2&pos=2&sid=fb562508-c3cb-4472-ad2f-8818e3aed141&sr=1.5016848) — AFI credits Robert De Niro as Michael Vronsky. It documents the film’s 1979 awards context, including De Niro’s Actor nomination and wins for picture, directing, supporting actor and sound. AFI also records sharply divided contemporary reactions and protests over the film’s depiction of Vietnamese people; this is reception history, not evidence that the film depicts events accurately.
2. [Academy: The 51st Academy Awards (1979)](https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/1979) — the official ledger honors 1978 releases. It lists De Niro as an Actor in a Leading Role nominee for *The Deer Hunter*, Christopher Walken as supporting-actor winner, and the film as winner for Best Picture, Directing, Film Editing and Sound. It lists a total of nine nominations and five wins.

**Safe article material:** Michael Vronsky is a fictional character. The award ledger supports categories and outcomes but does not establish plot or the historical accuracy of the film’s Vietnam-war representation. If discussing the reception controversy, attribute it to AFI’s record of contemporary responses rather than presenting any interpretation as fact.
