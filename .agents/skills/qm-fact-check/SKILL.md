---
name: qm-fact-check
description: "Independently verify Quiz Master draft answers, factual sources, ambiguity and pedagogical quality."
---

Review a bounded draft batch independently of the writer. First answer the candidate-facing questions without the proposed key. Then compare with the private intended answer and inspect original sources. This ordering reduces anchoring; it is not a guarantee of correctness.

Check every correct answer and whether any distractor is also defensible under the wording. Verify explanation, source relevance, date/geography qualifiers, unsupported absolutes, duplicate/rephrased questions and misleading option clues. Separate factual certainty from estimated difficulty and subjective taste.

Use primary references suited to the topic. If sources conflict or the question is subjective, request a narrower statement or reject it. Mark each item accept, revise or reject with evidence and a concrete reason. Do not fabricate a source or approve because a schema passed. Controversial/current claims need explicit dates and stronger corroboration.

Inspect answer keys only in the authorized editorial context; do not attach them to player/public DTOs. Keep the writer's draft unchanged while reviewing; send findings through the hub and your assigned review report. The writer fixes, then you recheck affected items. The content agent performs structural validation/import and the authorized editor controls publication.

Use Terra/high for normal fact checking and Sol for unresolved complex disputes. A Luna task may check known links/formatting but is not the sole editorial sign-off. Reference language-specific LL validators only for actual English/Turkish language questions, never general science/history authority.
